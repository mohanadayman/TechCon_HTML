History

TechCon began as a regional meetup in 2012 where engineers and designers gathered to share war stories and new tooling. Over the years it has grown into an international conference focusing on web platforms, cloud-native development, and applied AI. The event has expanded from a single day to a multi-track conference with workshops, hackathons, and hands-on labs.

Mission

Our mission is to create a welcoming, practical, and forward-looking environment for technologists to learn, collaborate, and accelerate innovation. We prioritize hands-on learning, inclusive discussion, and content that helps practitioners ship better products and services.
